# About Owl Eye Alert Platform

## 1. What is Owl Eye (OE)?

The **Owl Eye (OE) Alert Platform** is an active detection platform for **quality and compliance risks** based on AI artificial intelligence, visual algorithms, and process automation data integration.<br>

Through integration with business sources such as Maximo and MES, the system **automatically collects key process data including images, documents, and APQP deliverables**, uses AI algorithm models to identify anomalies in real-time, and precisely pushes alerts to responsible parties, achieving "**early problem detection, rapid responsibility assignment, and traceable closed-loop management**".
